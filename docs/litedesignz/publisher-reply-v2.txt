ABOUT SECTION
Soft garden florals and keepsakes made with care [48 chars]

LiteDesignz is for people who love soft garden florals, for those honouring or remembering someone they love, and for gift-givers looking for something meaningful and beautiful. It is also for anyone who enjoys printing at home or at a local print shop.

The look is an English cottage garden in warm golden light: climbing blush roses, cream hydrangeas, lavender and delphinium. It is a warm, gentle style, designed to feel like a keepsake. LiteDesignz is a faith-inspired shop, and that quiet sense of comfort shapes the work.

The first designs will be floral digital downloads in this garden style, with digital keepsakes and memorial designs planned for later. Everything is an instant digital download, so nothing is shipped and no physical item or frame is included. The designs are created with the help of AI design tools and finished by the shop.
