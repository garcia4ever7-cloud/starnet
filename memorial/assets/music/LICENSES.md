## Residual uncertainty (read this before selling)

- USAF Band tracks (05-08): public domain rests on the Air Force's own representation (its page is titled "Public Domain Music") and on 17 U.S.C. 105. That statute covers the United States; the U.S. government can in principle assert copyright abroad. The named arrangers (Chandler, Rawlins, Villanueva) are assumed to have arranged as Air Force members on duty - Commons tags the arrangements PD-USGov but says the metadata "has not necessarily been independently verified". The af.mil page itself could not be opened from this device (HTTP 403); only its heading was confirmed through the Wayback Machine, and its track list was not readable.
- Open Well-Tempered Clavier (02-03): artist text says CC0; Bandcamp's licence widget on the same page says CC BY 3.0. Credit the performer and either reading is satisfied.
- Musopen Chopin (04): CC0 is asserted by Musopen's own archive.org upload; the pianist's name and the Kickstarter terms were not verified.
- CC0 covers copyright and neighbouring rights only. It does not stop a wrongful Content ID claim; the uploader (the customer) would have to dispute it. Keep this file and the source URLs to answer one.
- Rejected candidates are listed in the research report, not stored here.
